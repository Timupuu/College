ERD diagram or Entity Relation diagram

  Entity: 
      > Teacher
      > Course
      > Student 
      > Class
      

Relations: 
    > One to One
       Teacher <--> Course
    
    > Many to one         OR One to many
       Student <--> Class     Class <--> Student

    > Many to many
      Teacher <--> Student

Business rule:
1. A Teacher oversees one entire course. (One to one)
2. Many students attain one class and do one course  (both are many to one) 
3. A Course is taught by one teacher, A course is taken by many students (one to one) (One to many)
4. A Class is attended by one teacher and attended by many students (one to one) (one to many)
