\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
\paragraph{During the last two decades in the feild of cybersecurity, many \nobreak incidents have taken place which has helped to bring the realm of cyber and infomation security to the place it is now}. \\Various data breaches and security threats have resulted in numerous new methods to prevet and stop such attacks. With the rapid development and reach of the internet growing ever so quickly it led to a collection of massive data including sensitive data. This also led criminals to find new tatctis to do cyber crimes.

\paragraph{With events such as the 2013 data breach of MySpace where 360 million users had their login credentials stolen, in the same year the personal information of 3 billion Yahoo users was compromised.} Still these events were not revealed by these cooperations for years. Adobe and LinkedIn were also breached, but the breach at Target would show that any feild was vulnerable to data breaches.

\paragraph{Target was third-largest retailor in United States and operated numerous stores within Canada and U.S upto 2000 stores.} It also ranked within the top 50 companies within the Fortune 500 index with millions of customers on a daily basis and a multinational chain.

\paragraph{The attack occured during holiday season via the POS i.e point of sale machine where the information of 70 million customer and the credit card of 40 million users were also compromised.}

\section{Unfolding}\label{sec:unfold}
\subsection{Security}\label{ssec:secu}
\paragraph{ Every cyber crime starts with a vulnerability.} Taget did comply with the Payment Card Industry (PCI) Security Standards, they had firewalls,Virtual local area networks(VLAN) along with Fire-Eye which provided multiple level of security from malware detection to network intrustion detection system (NIDS)
\subsection{Blunders}\label{ssec:blund}
\paragraph{Even though they had such level of protection they failed to detect as well as prevent some:}
\begin{itemize}
	\item They were unable to prevent the installation of malware into their POS units.
	\item They didn't properly look into the warinings generated by NIDS and FireEye as well as other tools
	\item They didn't follow proper protocol to segment their systems into different network ont the basis of sensitivity of data.
	\item They didn't have proper access control leading to privilege escalation
\end{itemize}
\subsection{Breach}\label{ssec:breach}
\subsubsection{Phase: 1 Infection }\label{sssec:p1}\paragraph{\indent Using phishing emails the attackers were able to infect Fazio's system.} What is beleived to be Citadel\footnote{A popular malware for stealing login info}
\paragraph{\indent After the access into the target's network through fazio mechanical the attackers were able to access Target's Ariba external billing system or the business section of Target.} These privileges were given to fazio so that they could upload their invoices. This was a huge vulnerability as the portal was not set to block executable files. Taking advantage of the access and the inability of fazio to interact with target's system the attackers were able to upload a "Web Shell"\footnote{A malicious script used to gain control on web servers}
\newpage
\subsubsection{Phase: 2 PoS Infection }\label{sssec:p2}\paragraph{\indent They named this "Webs shell to look like a authentic file, now they had remote control over the Target's servers.} They then located target's secure server due to the poor segementation done within their network. They then experimentated installing various malware on the PoS devices. They used a PoS malware called BlackPOS which is also refered to as Kaptoxa.

\subsubsection{Phase 3: Prvilege and extraction}\label{sssec:p3}
\paragraph{\indent After the acess and the successful hijacking of the PoS systems the attackers were just a few steps away from entering and extracting the data but they were in need of privilege i.e Domain Admin privileges.} They were able to easliy enter into the system as it had single sign on, a common design that allows administrators to login only once into their system and be logged in later on. Using Pass-the-hash method they were able to easily infeltrate the system as they only needed the hashes and not the cleartext passwords to login. Thus by only using hashes they were able to gain admin privileges into the system
\paragraph{\indent After succesfully gaining access they used those privileges to create their own seperate Domain Access account} Allowing them free access along with no suspicion. They again as before layed low naming their Domain Access account aftera legitimate IT application. They then took over three FTP servers\footnote{File Transfer Protocol}
\paragraph{\indent During peak time they would harvest the info stored in the PoS systems} Later sent to these FTP servers which would be passed onto other compromised machines and then to drop sites in Miami and Brazil. These data were later sold estimated to be around 11GB of data. They were later found on sale in black market fourms.

\section{Methodologies}\label{sec:met}
\paragraph{Here we will go indepth about the malware and exploits used and their functionalities:}
\begin{description}
	\item[\textit{BlackPOS} or \textit{Kaptoxa}]Initially seen on underground forums since Feb 2013, it is believed to be the major malware used in the data breaches at Target(2013),P.F Chang's(2013) \& Home Depot(2014). This mainly target Windows based POS machines. It would register itself as a Windows Service named "POSWDS" it would scan a list of processes and communicate with a compromised server (internal server repo) to upload the retrieved card informtaion.This fuction could only be enabled during the busy hours. They are analyzed in chunks and stored in a system ".dll" file which would periodically upload to the intrenal repo via NetBIOS\footnote{A old APi session layer that allows communication within a LAN} and SMB\footnote{Server Messege Block a protocol for shared access to files on a network}protocols\\
	\item[\textit{Citadel}] Citadel is a powerful banking trojan. Banking troans intercept communication with your bank to access your PINs\footnote{Persnal Identification Number} and TANs\footnote{Transaction Authentication Number}. Causing significant financial damage. It can be installed on a victim's computer with a drive-by-download attack most often using other exploit kits.Citadel then installs Reveton, a very bad ransomeware virus.These viruses then steal crucial finanxial data and login credentials while simultaneously making the computer a part of a botnet for other attacks.
	\item[\textit{Pass-the-hash}] Pash the hash is a cybersecurity attack where the attacker steals a "hashed" i.e kind of a session or login identifier and uses this stolen hash to create a new user session under the same privilege as the actual user. It bypasses the need for passwords only requiring the associated plaintext hash.
\end{description}
