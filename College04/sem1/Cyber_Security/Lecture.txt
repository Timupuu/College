  4. The type of cyber risks are as follows:
       
       Malware: A destructive and exploting script/ software that conducts various types of exploits and viruses

       Social engineering: The risk that is associated with the weakest link i.e humans where the trust and emotions are used as a basis of exploiting and causing cyber harm. 

       Identity and crednetial theft: The use of someone's private information for self gain by a ill intent without the person's consent or knowledge

       Network and system attacks: Attacks such as man in the middle, sql injection, denial of service where the cyber criminal uses various means intercept, exploit and cause harm to a service or weaken the availability of the services

       Cryptojacking: The unauthorized use of a user's end device to mine crypto currency causing load and stress on the end device without the person's knowledge.

  5. The types of malware are as follows: 
       
       Viruses: A type of malware that manipulates data or files within the end device to cause stress on the system and cause lagging and buffering

       Ransomware: A type of malware that steals/delets or controls the data of a user and demands ransom for it's release

       Spyware: A type of malware which takes control over the user's logs, input devices and captures and monitors the user's activities and his inputs
