1. Cyber risk are those things that pose a threat or potentially be dangerous to the intellectual online property, exploit data and compromise the confidentiality, availability and integrity of the data.

2. Factors that affect risk are:
  Probability
  Impact that will have significant  effect

3.We can quantify risk by using a percentage of risk which will help us to understand or visualize the probability or the chance that iy may occur

4. We can calculate risk by using a very simple formula 
    impact * probability = cost (risk)

    risk = likelihood of occurance of vulnerability * value of information asset - % of risk mitigated by current  controls + uncertainity of current knowledge of vulnerability

5. Steps to measure impact of risk
  Direct cost
  Indirect cost

6. Different risk management methods
  Avoidane: stop using outdated software
  Reduction: Regular software update
  transfer: cyber insurance
  Acceptance Budgting for minor risks
